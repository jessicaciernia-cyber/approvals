Telehealth brands don't fail on demand. They fail on backend.

Traffic is the easy part. Attention is buyable. The rest is not.

Four things have to be true before a single order can be taken: a pharmacy relationship, platform and intake, a provider network, and LegitScript certification.

LegitScript is the hard gate. It sits between a live site and the ability to process payments or advertise at all. Nothing downstream happens without it.

The sequence is not negotiable. Website, then certification, then payment processing, then ads. Brands that run these in parallel pay for traffic they cannot convert.

A stalled launch does not cost the build fee. It costs the months of spend and momentum burned while the backend catches up.

A clinic and a platform are not the same thing. One serves patients. The other serves patients at a rate that does not depend on you.
