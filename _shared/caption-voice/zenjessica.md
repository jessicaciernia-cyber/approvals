## The five pillars

Every story post belongs to one. They rotate in order so no two consecutive posts land in the
same pillar.

| # | Pillar | The line it lives under | What it earns her |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Little but mighty** | "Jesse, you're little, but you're mighty" | Origin. Why nothing intimidates her. |
| 2 | **I didn't know I was allowed to get up** | The fall at state finals | Permission. The single most reusable idea she owns. |
| 3 | **Two steps ahead** | "You only have to be two steps ahead of somebody that needs you" | The actual mechanics of how she started. Converts. |
| 4 | **Mom is the superpower** | "Being a mom is one of my superpowers" | The audience. Nurse practitioners and founder moms. |
| 5 | **Say it so they understand** | The vow in her grandfather's hospital room | Why she is a clinician who markets, not a marketer who found healthcare. |

**Pillar 2 is the flagship.** Do not spend it early and do not spend it twice. It is the one
that should carry the first real DM funnel.

## Rules for this lane

1. **The stories run in her voice, not documentary voice.** The episode has a narrator. Do not
   copy the narrator. Every post above is her speaking.
2. **No story post carries a business CTA in the first three lines.** The keyword goes at the
   bottom or it isn't there. A story that pitches in line two reads as an ad and gets scrolled.
3. **Keyword posts need the DM built first.** Four here need one: GETUP, TWO, PLAIN, SLIDE.
   That is four deliverables, not four posts.
   **Chapter 3 carries no keyword at all,** by design. Its job is trust, not capture.
4. **Pillar 2 is not renewable.** There is one fall at state finals. Do not tell it twice in a
   quarter and do not let it become a metaphor she reaches for in captions.
5. **Verify quotes against the video before burning them into a graphic.** These came from
   auto-captions. See the discrepancy list in the intel file.
6. **Compliance still applies.** Post 12 touches conditions and treatments. It names no drug,
   claims no outcome, and describes people, not results. Keep it that way.
7. **Personal is still hers.** The one unplanned slot a week does not become a story slot. The
   planned lane exists so the unplanned one stays unplanned.

## How the captions read

First person, her speaking, short lines with air between them. Concrete details: a place, a number, a thing someone said. The turn comes late. A story post never sells in the first three lines. Ends with one comment prompt, then the hashtag line exactly as on the site: `#welliemd #therealzenjessica #growpro`.

## Flag vocabulary

Same two levels as WellieMD. `check` = a claim, name, or quote that should be verified against the source (the episode, the person) before it ships. `stop` = something the lane rules bar outright, such as a business CTA in the first three lines or a quote not verified against the video.
